## Methodology

- **Puzzle set**: 200 puzzles (100 from top1465 SE 8–10 range; 100 from forum_hardest_1905_11plus SE 10+ range).

## Verdict

The overall RMS gap of 1.33 SE units has two components: (a) **calibration bias** — per-technique base-rating offsets from SE ground truth, correctible by applying the weight edits above; (b) **coverage gaps** — techniques we don't implement (Region FC, Dynamic FC, Nested AIC) cause 83% of our T4Plus puzzles to have SE ER > 9.0, which our sentinel at 7.5 systematically underestimates by 1.6 SE units on average. Applying the per-technique mean corrections reduces RMS from 1.33 to 0.46 (corrected residual). The remaining gap is dominated by coverage — new techniques are needed for the SE 9.5–12+ range, not just weight tuning.
