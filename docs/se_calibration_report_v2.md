# SE Calibration Report

Generated by `scripts/compare_serate_vs_ours.py`.

## Methodology

- **Puzzle set**: 200 puzzles (100 from top1465 SE 8–10 range; 100 from forum_hardest_1905_11plus SE 10+ range).
- **SE rater**: 1to9only Java fork compiled from source, `diuf.sudoku.test.serate`.
- **Our rater**: `rate-batch --mode full` (Rust, release build).
- **Join key**: exact 81-char puzzle string.
- **Metric compared**: SE `ED` field (ER = overall hardest technique) vs our `se_score` = max(base_rating) across fired frontier techniques.

## Dataset

- serate produced 128 valid ratings out of 200 submitted (0 invalid/skipped).
- Our rater: 200 records (200 submitted, 0 errors).
- Joined (both rated): **128 puzzles**.
- Not rated by serate (hit time cap or still pending): 72 puzzles.

## Verdict

Phase A–D work partially closed the calibration gap (phase 1 RMS was 1.79; now **1.53** on 128
joined puzzles). The corrected RMS (0.56) shows the gap is primarily **systematic bias**, not
random variance — the per-technique mean offsets dominate. Key findings:

1. **RegionFC (Phase C) base rating 7.6 is too high**: mean bias −1.02 over 27 puzzles. SE assigns
   these 6.0–8.2 on average. Suggested correction: 7.6 → 6.6. Also has 1 false-positive trigger.
2. **NestedFC (Phase D) base rating 9.5 is too high**: mean bias −1.19 over 15 puzzles. SE 9.2–11.1
   range; our 9.5 flat overestimates the easier end. Suggested correction: 9.5 → 8.3.
3. **ALS-XZ / AIC (Phase B) calibration is good**: mean diff +0.03 over 22 puzzles (virtually zero).
4. **16-puzzle hard residual** (SE 11.0–11.1, empty frontier): requires Phase E (NestedFC level=3).
5. **DFC/CFC buckets**: n=1 each — not enough puzzles in our calib set for reliable estimates.

Overall: Phase A–D reduced RMS from 1.79 → 1.53 — modest improvement, dominated by the
RegionFC/NestedFC base rating overestimates. Applying the two weight edits above should
bring RMS to ~0.8–0.9. The 16-puzzle residual at SE 11.x is a pure coverage issue, not weights.
