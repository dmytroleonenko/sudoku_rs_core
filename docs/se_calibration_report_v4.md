### Interpretation

1. **NestedFC L4 (new bucket):** 10 puzzles previously classified as `none` (unsolved, top_tech=none) or `nested_forcing_chain_l3` are now correctly solved by L4, reducing the `none` bucket from 5→2 and `nested_forcing_chain_l3` from 17→10. L4 bias of −0.54 is moderate and expected (SE 10.5 is L4's rating; actual SE ER range is 11.0–11.1 for these puzzles, ~0.5 gap). This is the primary RMS driver for the 0.081 improvement.

2. **AIC XY/X split:** No visible effect at the JSONL frontier level — the split is internal to the Rust rater and doesn't change the exported technique label (`aic`). The `aic` bucket has 0 top-technique records in both v3 and v4 (AIC always co-fires with higher-rated techs on this puzzle set). No measurable calibration impact in this dataset.

3. **CellFC k=2 → 6.6:** No change in per-bucket stats (CellFC n=33, mean=−0.82 identical). The k=2 puzzles in this set are likely all solved before CFC fires (the CFC cascade only reaches k=2 on intermediate steps, not as the top technique). No regression.

4. **No regressions:** All buckets either improved or held steady. No bucket became worse in v4 vs v3.

5. **Corrected RMS identical (0.458):** The residual after per-bucket mean subtraction is unchanged — the within-bucket scatter is structural (coverage gap), not calibration noise. Weight tuning alone cannot close it; new techniques (DFC, full NFC L5+) are required.

## Verdict

The overall RMS gap of 1.25 SE units has two components: (a) **calibration bias** — per-technique base-rating offsets from SE ground truth, correctible by applying the weight edits above; (b) **coverage gaps** — techniques we don't implement (Region FC, Dynamic FC, Nested AIC) cause 83% of our T4Plus puzzles to have SE ER > 9.0, which our sentinel at 7.5 systematically underestimates by 1.5 SE units on average. Applying the per-technique mean corrections reduces RMS from 1.25 to 0.46 (corrected residual). The remaining gap is dominated by coverage — new techniques are needed for the SE 9.5–12+ range, not just weight tuning.
